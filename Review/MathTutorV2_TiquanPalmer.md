Overall Thoughts of the Assignment
The assignment itself was very interesting. Since i have never worked with any kind of coding
or programing like this before, i was very anxious and paranoid about messing up and leaving
my partner to do all the work and pick up my slack. Thankfully, the instructions were very easy
to follow. I was able to install CLion onto my computer and navigate it. I was able to connect my
github account to my CLion account on my personal computer which made it easy to do some
code at home and to have access to the project at all times. I like the idea behind the project
and while somewhat stressful, it was a lot of fun seeing how the code turned out.
The Good
The assignment was very straight forward. Debbie made sure that the instructions were
simple, provided bonus videos to accompany our lecture recordings. We also had a fair bit of
somewhat experienced coders in the class so help from classmates outside of class time was
great. The patience was also a net positive for our assignment. We got extra time to work on it
and clean up any mess made to ensure that the project looked to the best of it’s ability. Debbie
was also very helpful in that she would always find the hiccups of the code quickly whether it be
a simple glance at our monitor and suggestion, or meeting outside of class to help me
understand what exactly i am supposed to do. The easiest part of the assignment for me was
inserting the jokes because i am a very silly person so i wanted to be as creative as possible
with my google searches for puns. My partner was also very good at finding puns online as well.
I would hope that our puns are the greatest puns from the projects in class!
The Bad
My CLion account on my personal computer and on the class monitor just didn’t agree. My
monitor had the same accounts and emails yet when the project was being worked on, i could
never see any of the progress that was made by my partner or myself. It was always the work
done by the beginning portion of the assignment. I also couldn’t do “Pull” and “Push” on the
monitor as well because of this. I was able to use my personal and she ran better than i
expected, but i will try to get this problem fixed so that it doesn’t become a full semester thing.
Another is the access to bonus videos. I tend to use the Ecosia browser instead of chrome
because i am most comfortable with that specific browser. I can’t access the bonus videos or
the class lectures on that browser, and when using chrome, Github runs a bit slower than on the
Ecosia Browser as well. That was really tricky to deal with because having two browsers open
simultaneously so i can do the project and watch the lecture and bonus videos did drain my
battery and give me less overall time at home to do the project.
The Ugly
Outside of just generally feeling like i was sandbagging my partner the entire assignment, i cant
really think of anything from the assignment i’d say was downright ugly. The README portion of
the assignment was probably the hardest part of the assignment for me because i just couldn’t
seem to get the headings and subheadings coded properly. I ultimately had to ask for help after
several attempts on my own and Debbie did assist me in that regard. I still struggle with the
pushing and committing to push from CLion. I also need to start paying more attention to the
symbology i use because i tend to mix up my “cin” and my “cout” and because i do a lot of
typing in geography classes, i have the habit of just shifting on the colon key by reflex. When
doing “endl;”, specifically during the joke codes, my partner had to tell me repeatedly that i was
shifting for a colon instead of ending the line with a semi colon. That will be problematic going
forward.
Final Thoughts
For my first ever assignment involving computer code, I think this was a success. It has left a
fair bit of intimidation for what is to be expected for future assignments, but i’m also very excited
to see what i will be doing next. This assignment was very eye opening into the expectations of
this class as well, and keeping up with the Zybook helped a ton when it came to remembering
“cin” vs “cout”.
